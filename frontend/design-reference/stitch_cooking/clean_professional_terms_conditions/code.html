Terms of Engagement & Professional Obligations

As a client of The Modern Archivist, your engagement is governed by the professional standards set forth by the Institute of Chartered Accountants and national regulatory statutes.

Section 01: Legal Provisions Last Updated: October 2024

1. Definitions and Interpretation

In these Terms and Conditions, "The Firm", "We", "Us" refers to The Modern Archivist. "The Client", "You" refers to the entity or individual engaging our professional services. "Engagement" refers to the specific scope of work defined in the accompanying Letter of Engagement.

2. Professional Standards

We will provide our services with professional rigor and in accordance with the ethical requirements of the accounting profession. This includes maintaining independence, integrity, and objectivity in all audits and advisory capacities.

You acknowledge that you are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the financial records and information provided to us. We do not assume responsibility for the detection of fraud or errors unless specifically stated in a dedicated forensic audit engagement.

3. Fees and Billing Cycles

All professional fees are calculated based on the intellectual density of the work performed, the level of expertise required, and the time expended. Invoices are issued monthly and are payable within 14 business days. Late payments may result in the suspension of all advisory services.

4. Confidentiality and Data Privacy

The Modern Archivist maintains a strict "Zero-Leak" policy. Your financial data is protected under military-grade encryption protocols and is never shared with third parties without express written consent or legal compulsion under regulatory audit.

5. Limitation of Liability

Our liability for any loss or damage arising out of this engagement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall be limited to the total fees paid by the client for the specific service giving rise to the claim. This limitation does not apply to instances of proven gross negligence or willful misconduct.

6. Termination

Either party may terminate the engagement by providing thirty (30) days' written notice. Upon termination, all outstanding fees become immediately due and payable. We will return all original documents but reserve the right to retain copies as required by archival statutes.
