Terms of Engagement & Professional Obligations

Review the following framework governing the provision of professional accounting services, compliance standards, and fiduciary responsibilities.

1. Scope of Professional Services

The Archivist ("the Firm") shall provide accounting, taxation, and advisory services as specifically outlined in the individual Letter of Engagement provided to the Client. These services are performed in accordance with the International Standard on Quality Management (ISQM) and applicable regional regulatory frameworks.

The Firm's responsibility is limited to the expression of an opinion based on the audit or review conducted. We do not guarantee the absolute detection of fraud or error unless specifically commissioned for forensic investigation.

2. Client Responsibilities & Data Integrity

The Client acknowledges that the reliability of the Firm’s output is contingent upon the accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of the information provided by the Client. The Client warrants that all financial records and supporting documentation submitted are authentic and represent a true and fair view of the entity's financial position.

Critical Notice

Failure to disclose material financial information may result in the immediate termination of the engagement without refund of retainers.

3. Confidentiality & Fiduciary Care

All information acquired during the course of the engagement regarding the Client’s business affairs shall be treated as strictly confidential. The Firm maintains rigorous encryption standards and secure data archival protocols ("The Vault") to ensure the protection of sensitive intellectual property.

Disclosure will only occur if mandated by law, professional standard, or where explicit written consent is granted by the Client.

4. Professional Fees & Disbursements

Fees are calculated based on the complexity of the engagement and the seniority of the professional staff assigned. Out-of-pocket expenses incurred specifically for the engagement will be billed at cost plus a 5% administrative handling fee. Standard payment terms are net 14 days from the date of invoice issuance.

5. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Firm's total liability for any loss or damage arising out of or in connection with the services provided, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall be limited to three times the total fees paid by the Client for the specific engagement giving rise to the claim.
